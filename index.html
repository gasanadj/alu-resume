Public health / Rwanda Biomedical Centre

Less waiting.
More time for care.

I worked on a youth-health survey platform across 10 centers, optimizing live fingerprint duplicate checks so teams could keep collection moving.

<5sec

At peak, down from
approximately 10 minutes.

The engineering story +

Live duplicate checks had become a bottleneck in data collection. I optimized that check from roughly 10 minutes to under 5 seconds at peak, helping centers hit their collection targets.

The important outcome was the time returned to the people using the system. This figure describes the duplicate-check step, not the entire survey.

✓ A faster check. A smoother collection.

Immersive learning / VLearn

Don’t just learn it.
Step inside it.

I built curriculum-aligned VR experiments with a conversational AI tutor and offline-capable learning experiences. Evaluated with 20 students.

Explore the learning experience +

Built with Unity and C#, VLearn explores subjects such as gravity, molecular assembly, the digestive system, and the solar system through interactive experiments.

The evaluation involved 20 students; it is an early evaluation, not a claim of proven learning gains at scale. Offline capability applies to the learning experiences, rather than a promise that every AI feature works without a connection.
